# Analysis-output retention policy

The repository-local `analyses/` directory holds expensive or downstream-
required artifacts, not every table produced by the notebook.

## Retention rule

Save an output here only when it is expensive to recreate, required by a later
analysis step, or a durable quality-control audit. Easily regenerated
descriptive/statistical tables should be generated by the notebook when
needed, rather than retained. Every retained artifact must live in a documented
subfolder.

## Git publication

At the user's request, result tables, generated reports, caches and audit
evidence remain local. The `analyses/`, `exploratory_results/` and `reports/`
directories and the generated audit reports in `doc/` are Git-ignored.
Previously tracked analysis artifacts are removed from the Git index without
deleting local files. Source code, notebooks, analysis settings, tests and
method documentation are versioned; notebooks are published without cell
outputs or execution counts. A fresh clone needs access to the configured
study data to regenerate results and checkpoints. Recovery archives and
figure export directories retain their existing Git-ignore rules. Links to
local audit reports are available only in the working copy where those
reports have been generated.

## `qc/`

Full-cohort longitudinal EEG preprocessing audit, retained because it requires
filtering and epoching every eligible run.

- `longitudinal_preprocessing_qc_subjects.csv` — final per-participant status.
- `longitudinal_preprocessing_qc_runs.csv` — run-level Status, epoch, and
  baseline checks.
- `longitudinal_preprocessing_qc_failures.csv` — strict QC exceptions needing
  follow-up.

Missing training-response audit, regenerated by the final notebook section
using `posthoc_analysis.rt_qc` (findings in `training_response_qc.md`):

- `training_missing_response_missing_trials.csv` — one row per absent GDF
  response, including source run, feedback, text RT, pooled `rt_ms`, original
  `gdf_rt_ms`, provenance, and raw checks. A text fallback does not remove a row
  from this acquisition audit.
- `training_missing_response_affected_runs.csv` — affected-run Status codes
  and recorded marker widths, which are not physical pulse durations.
- `training_missing_response_status_segments.csv` — lossless run-length
  encoding of Status from 50 ms before each missing-response stimulus through
  the next stimulus/end; sample stops are exclusive.
- `training_missing_response_by_*.csv` — frozen audit summaries by subject,
  session, group/session, stimulus condition, feedback, trial position, and
  text-RT band, retaining full-cohort denominators.

## `eeg_pd/behavior_filtered/`

Expensive trial-filtered Pd rebuild and durable exclusion/alignment QC, generated
by `posthoc_analysis.pd_filtered.rebuild_behavior_filtered_pd()`:

- `subject_session_pd.csv`: 768-sample participant/session waveforms, pooling
  retained distractor trials equally across all available runs and both sides.
- `trial_exclusion_audit.csv`: original corrected training rows, mutually
  exclusive exclusion reasons, and the exact `retained_for_pd` mask.
- `exclusion_summary.csv`, `rt_bounds.csv`: participant/session exclusion counts
  and single-pass mean ±3 sample-SD RT bounds pooling all stimulus conditions.
- `run_trial_counts.csv`: source GDF paths and original/retained distractor
  counts, including retained left/right counts.
- `provenance.json`: behavioral-content and analysis-code hashes, source-file
  paths/sizes/mtimes, and output checksums. Changed inputs invalidate the cache.

Positive-AUC summaries/statistics remain regenerated in the notebook; they are
not saved as extra repository tables. The existing unfiltered condition-average
CSV and cluster-permutation results are historical inputs/results and are not
updated by this rebuild.

## `session5_feature_references/`

Reserved for the compact, frozen Session-5 top-30 reference artifacts needed
for longitudinal feature tracking:

- `session5_reference_summary.parquet`
- `session5_reference_clean_trials.parquet`
- `session5_reference_top30.parquet`
- `session5_reference_settings.json`
- `session5_reference_build_log.parquet` — checkpointed per-participant,
  per-decoder build status, selected pruning iteration, CV AUPRC, and any
  failure message. Retained as the model-selection audit trail.
- `longitudinal_top30_r2.parquet` — final long-format feature-tracking result
  table. It contains both run-wise r2 and session-wise r2 calculated after
  pooling eligible trials across runs before r2 estimation. This is retained
  because it requires applying frozen transforms to all raw EEG runs.
- `longitudinal_top30_r2_build_log.parquet` — checkpointed participant-level
  status for the raw-EEG frozen-reference evaluation. Retained to document
  complete and failed evaluations without inferring missing rows as zero.
- `longitudinal_top30_r2_session5_training_post_build_log.parquet` — audit of
  the atomic append of full-dataset, four-run Session-5 training r² estimates.
  These rows are labelled `session5_training_post` in the main r² table and
  are selection-linked descriptive values, not independent performance data.
- `transforms/{subject_id}_{decoder_side}_frozen_reference.npz` — one compact
  right and left transform per participant. Each contains the final
  Session-5 xDAWN filters, epoch-time and resampling indices, z-score means
  and standard deviations, selected feature indices, and channel order. These
  transforms must be applied unchanged to Session-1 training and decoding-run
  data; they must not be refit during longitudinal evaluation.

## `eeg_pd/seven_point/`

Expensive decoding-session Pd waveforms for the seven-point 200–500 ms AUC
figure, generated by `posthoc_analysis.pd_longitudinal`. Each
`{subject}_session{1..5}.npz` stores the 768-sample mean sensor Pd and time grid;
the matching JSON binds raw-file sizes/mtimes, analysis-code hashes, output
checksum, run counts, and preprocessing warnings. Partial completed sessions
can be reused after an interrupted build. `run_trial_counts.csv` includes all
included decoding runs and the missing-EEG audit. Participant AUC/group
summaries are regenerated in the notebook, not retained as additional tables.

Use every distractor trial from explicitly non-practice-named decoding folders,
with no behavioral, feedback, or artifact exclusions. The user explicitly
approved keeping all five e27 Session-1 runs and all seven e22/e37 Session-5
runs; e52 Session 3 contributes available EEG with its missing GDF reported.
The plotted pre/post endpoints reuse `behavior_filtered/` unchanged.

## `eeg_pd/pre_decoder_conditions/`

Expensive all-trial Session 1 training sensor waveforms, generated by
`posthoc_analysis.pre_decoder_waveforms.build_pre_decoder_waveforms()` for the
requested right/left decoder class comparisons. This separate analysis includes
all non-practice trials without behavioral or artifact exclusions; it does not
replace the behavior-filtered Pd endpoints.

- `{subject}.npz`: 3 × 768 PO8−PO7 mean waveforms (no distractor, right distractor,
  left distractor), their trial counts, and the canonical time grid.
- `{subject}.json`: raw GDF/text source sizes/mtimes, code hashes, NPZ checksum,
  run counts, and preprocessing warnings. Includes the documented e30 seven-run
  pre-session; other run-count mismatches fail validation.

Trials are weighted equally within each participant/condition; plotted means
and SEM weight participants equally across both groups. The right comparison
negates both class means to display PO7−PO8; the left displays PO8−PO7. The same
all-position no-distractor trials appear in both panels with opposite signs.
These are sensor waveforms before class balancing, pruning, or xDAWN, not the
final decoder feature vectors. Figures are exported as
`figures/pre_decoder_condition_waveforms.png` and `.pdf` with poster typography.

## `eeg_theta/posterior_seven_point/`

Expensive side-specific posterior theta checkpoints generated by
`posthoc_analysis.posterior_theta` and the final **Posterior theta across pre,
five intervention sessions, and post** notebook section. The original
midfrontal analysis supplies an explicitly configured, checksum-validated
inventory, settings, and assessment trial mask. Default reference:
`exploratory_results/theta_paper_reproduction/` within this repository.
Set notebook environment variable `PD_THETA_REFERENCE_ROOT` if relocated.

- `source_inventory.csv`, `trial_mask.csv`, `settings.json`,
  `input_validation.json`: validated source identities, phase-specific trial
  retention and settings/provenance. Raw source size/mtime and paired-file
  hashes must match before reuse.
- `runs/{assessment,intervention}/*.npz` and matching JSON: linear baseline
  sums and stimulus power sums by no/right/left distractor, at PO7/P7/P5 and
  PO8/P8/P6. Shapes are 6 × 9 and 3 × 6 × 9 × 205. Store retained trial IDs,
  frequency/time axes, counts, source/code/mask signatures, and file checksums.
- `run_log*.csv`, `*validation.json`, `*.log`: durable extraction/QA audits.
  Pre/post pooled values must reproduce existing full-scalp posterior theta
  summaries; an intervention run must reproduce the original midfrontal
  two-condition sufficient statistics when the new side bins are pooled.

Preserve the original theta inventory: 383 assessment runs, 21,940 retained
assessment trials; 1,150 intervention runs, all 69,000 trials retained. This
uses the theta-specific e22/e37 first-folder exclusions, all five e27 Session-1
runs, and the missing e52 Session-3 Run-6 exception, rather than the later Pd
trajectory inventory. No participant-loss criterion is added. Full 61-channel
Laplacian precedes ROI selection. Pool linear power across trials and runs
before normalizing to each session's common all-retained-trial baseline and
converting to dB; then average target samples, frequencies, and ROI electrodes.

Participant/group summaries are regenerated in memory. Three five-panel
figures are exported to `figures/posterior_theta/` in PDF/SVG/PNG, with shared
y-axis limits, 16 participants per group, and mean ± SEM. No statistical tests.

The appended **Posterior theta: Group × Session × Trial type × EEG side ANOVA**
section regenerates statistics from these caches without changing them.
`anova_validation.json` retains input/code hashes, software versions, model
membership/settings, SS-partition checks and maximum independent numerical
errors for the seven-point, five-session intervention and pre/post models.
Ordinary ANOVA tables, descriptives and exact participant inputs remain in the
notebook/result dictionaries rather than new repository CSVs.

The subsequent **Posterior theta: Group × distractor presence × intervention
session** section reuses pooled bilateral summaries for a 2 × 2 × 5 ANOVA.
Its seven-effect table, descriptives, numerical audit and input/code hashes
remain in the executed notebook/result dictionary; no additional cache or
ordinary statistics CSV is created.

The **Posterior theta: exploratory Group × Condition follow-ups** section
reuses that ANOVA's exact inputs. Four simple effects, participant condition
means/contrasts, the reconstructed interaction, and validation/provenance are
notebook/result-dictionary outputs only; no additional retained cache files.

## Rerun and correctness audit (2026-11-05)

`analyses/qc/rerun_2026-11-05/` retains the user-requested verification of the
selected analyses. See `analysis_validation_2026-11-05.md` for scope, corrections,
results, and limitations. Its subdirectories are:

- `training_inputs/`: full raw-GDF reconstruction and comparison of all 22,980
  training trials, plus sample-level checks of the 47 absent responses.
- `behavior_bci/`: behavioral, BCI, feedback/RT, intervention behavior and
  Color/Shape reruns; independent statistical checks; explicit before/after
  tables for corrected tests; raw-input comparisons.
- `pd_decoder/`: Pd, historical sensitivity/response controls, frozen feature
  references and longitudinal feature audit, with extraction/model comparisons.
- `theta/`: assessment/intervention/posterior/Stroop theta, source/checkpoint
  integrity, raw recomputation, statistical and figure checks.
- `integration/`: executed notebook-cell logs/source hashes, static notebook
  validation, environment versions, test logs and the corrected EEG demo check.

Audit tables are durable QC evidence, not an additional family of scientific
tests. Retain comparison reports, source/output hashes, scripts and diagnostics;
remove duplicate audit-only extraction blobs after verified agreement with the
canonical expensive cache. Audit figures live in `figures/audit_2026-11-05/`.

`analyses/qc/manuscript_replication_2026-11-05/` holds the subsequent claim-level
replication of manuscript Results 3.1–3.5 and the user-requested theta–RT
direction update. It retains exact-versus-reported comparison tables, executed
drivers, source/output hashes, and proof that switching from RT change to RT
improvement changes signs but not observations, absolute correlations or
two-sided p values. Current theta–RT results use pre-minus-post RT improvement;
the earlier `rerun_2026-11-05` audit records the previous post-minus-pre
convention as historical evidence.
